NovaCure Pharma — TRAINING DOCUMENT (FICTIONAL)
Document ID: SOP-LOT-QUARANTINE-001
Title: Lot Quarantine After Packaging Integrity Complaint
Version: 1.0 (Training Prototype Only)
Effective Date: 2026-01-15
Classification: Synthetic / Non-Clinical / Non-Regulatory

1. PURPOSE
This fictional SOP describes intake-desk steps when a packaging integrity
complaint is recorded for a finished pack. It does NOT assess medical
causality, treatment, or regulatory reportability.

2. SCOPE
Applies only to fictional Product Quality Complaints involving:
- broken seals
- crushed cartons
- damaged blisters
- unreadable labels

3. LOT IDENTIFIER FORMAT
All lot numbers in this training system must follow: LOT-XXXXX
Example: LOT-88392

4. PROCEDURE
4.1 Confirm the complaint is saved in the Intake Tracker with a REF-YYYY-XXXX ID.
4.2 Record product name and batch/lot exactly as provided.
4.3 Quarantine the implicated fictional lot within 4 business hours.
4.4 Notify the fictional Quality Desk mailbox: qc-desk@novacure.example
4.5 Add an audit note that quarantine was requested (training log only).
4.6 Do not provide medical advice in intake notes.

5. RELATED EXAMPLES
- Packaging seal broken on NeuroCalm 50mg, LOT-88392
- Crushed shipper for CardioSafe 10mg, LOT-12045

6. OUT OF SCOPE
- Causality assessment
- Treatment recommendations
- Regulatory submission decisions
- Real patient personal data

END OF DOCUMENT
