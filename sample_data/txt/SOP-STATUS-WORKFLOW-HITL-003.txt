NovaCure Pharma — TRAINING DOCUMENT (FICTIONAL)
Document ID: SOP-STATUS-WORKFLOW-HITL-003
Title: Status Lifecycle and Human-in-the-Loop Close Gate
Version: 1.0 (Training Prototype Only)
Effective Date: 2026-02-15
Classification: Synthetic / Non-Clinical / Non-Regulatory

1. PURPOSE
Define allowed status transitions for fictional intake records.

2. ALLOWED STATUS VALUES
- New
- In Review
- Closed

3. ALLOWED TRANSITIONS
- New -> In Review
- In Review -> Closed (ONLY after Human-in-the-Loop approval)

4. FORBIDDEN TRANSITIONS
- New -> Closed
- Closed -> New
- Closed -> In Review

5. HITL CLOSE GATE
When a reviewer requests Close from In Review:
5.1 Case is marked pending_close but remains In Review.
5.2 A second human approver must confirm close.
5.3 Approval is written to the local audit trail.
5.4 Only then status becomes Closed.

6. WORKED EXAMPLE
REF-2026-0003 (PainAway Gel label smudge):
- Created as New
- Moved to In Review by Arjun
- Close requested by Arjun
- HITL approved by Priya
- Final status Closed

7. NOTE
Closing a training record is an intake triage completion flag only.
It is NOT a medical or regulatory decision.

END OF DOCUMENT
